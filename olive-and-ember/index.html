❧ Our Roots & Heritage

Rooted in Flavor. Inspired by Togetherness.

Bliss & Bloom began with a simple idea: great food should bring people closer. Inspired by seasonal ingredients, creative cooking, and the warmth of shared meals, we created a place where every detail matters — from the first bite to the final goodbye.

Founded in Jaipur’s quiet garden district, our culinary identity bridges traditional hearth-side roasting with contemporary world flavors. We partner with regenerative farms across Rajasthan for heirloom produce, press our olive oils in small batches, and let our sourdough rest in deep cedar boxes.

Thoughtfully Selected Ingredients

Fresh, regional, and traceable produce picked at peak flavor from Rajasthan farms.

Creative Seasonal Cooking

A dynamic menu evolving alongside seasonal harvests and wood-fired hearth techniques.

Hospitality from the Heart

Attentive, gracious service designed to make you feel right at home.
